Delete your Apni Gari account and data

You can delete your account in the app: Settings → Privacy and legal → Delete account. The app will ask you to sign in again and confirm the deletion.

If you cannot access the app, email apnigari25@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account with the subject “Delete my Apni Gari account”. We will verify the request and aim to complete it within 30 days. Do not email your password or one-time codes.

What deletion removes

When an in-app deletion succeeds, we delete the account and associated profile, listings, stories, favorites, push tokens, uploaded files, voice notes, and related chat records from active systems where supported by the deployed database schema. A minimal deletion-request record may be retained without an account link. Limited safety or legal records and provider backups may remain for a limited period under the provider’s plan and settings.
