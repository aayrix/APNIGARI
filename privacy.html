Apni Gari Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026 · Version 1.0

Who operates Apni Gari

Apni Gari is operated by Aliyan Abdullah as an individual. Contact: apnigari25@gmail.com. Location: Mianwali, Pakistan. Website: apnigari.online.

Information we collect

  • Account and profile information: email address, name, phone number, city, profile photo, and account settings. Supabase Auth handles passwords; Apni Gari does not receive or store a readable password.
  • Marketplace content: vehicle details, price, description, city or chosen location, listing photos, and information shown on your seller profile.
  • Social and communication content: stories, connection and favorite records, reports, text and group messages, shared images, voice notes, and locations you choose to share in a chat. Stories are shown in the app for up to 24 hours.
  • Call information: call signaling and information needed to connect participants. Voice calls use Agora. The app does not record calls.
  • Device and technical information: push notification token, app and device information needed to deliver notifications and operate the service, and technical logs generated by service providers, which may include IP address.
  • Location: if you grant permission, the app can use your current location to find nearby listings or share a current or live location in a chat. A location shared in a chat is visible to its participants.

We do not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs in the app. We do not access your contacts. Call features may use Android system permissions needed to provide call alerts and calls.

App permissions

  • Camera and photos: take or choose listing, profile, or story images.
  • Microphone: record voice notes and use voice calls when you choose those features.
  • Location: find nearby listings or share your location when you choose.
  • Notifications: deliver message and call alerts.
  • Call alerts: Android may use full-screen notification and foreground-service permissions to show incoming call alerts and maintain an active call.

You can change permissions in your device settings. Features that need a permission may not work when it is denied.

How information is used and shared

We use information to create and secure accounts, operate listings and profiles, connect users, deliver messages and calls, provide location features and notifications, respond to reports, prevent abuse, and maintain the service.

Active listings and seller profiles can be visible to people who are not signed in. A public seller profile may show your name, phone number, city, and profile photo. Do not add information to a listing or profile that you do not want other people to see. Chat and group messages are available to their participants. Messages are stored so they can be delivered and synchronized; they are not end-to-end encrypted and may be accessed by the service operator when needed to investigate abuse, provide support, or comply with law. Other users may retain information they already saw or copied.

We use these providers to run app features:

  • Supabase: account authentication, database, file storage, realtime updates, and server functions.
  • Cloudflare R2: listing photo storage and delivery.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: push notifications.
  • Resend: delivery of account verification and password recovery emails through Supabase Auth.
  • Agora: voice calling.
  • OpenStreetMap tile services: map images. If you open directions, your device may hand the location to a maps app such as Google Maps under that app’s own terms.

These providers process information as needed to provide their services. Depending on the provider and the selected hosting region, information may be processed or stored outside Pakistan. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and service information while an account is active. You can delete your account in the app under Settings → Privacy and legal → Delete account. The app asks you to sign in again and confirm. When deletion succeeds, the service deletes the account and associated profile, listings, stories, favorites, push tokens, uploaded files, voice notes, and related chat records from active systems where supported by the deployed database schema. A minimal deletion-request record may be retained to record completion, with the account link removed. Information in a provider’s backups may remain until those backups expire under that provider’s plan and settings. Reports or records needed for safety or legal obligations may be retained in limited form.

If you cannot access the app, email apnigari25@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my Apni Gari account”. We will verify the request and aim to complete it within 30 days. Do not email your password or one-time codes.

For a copy or correction of your information, contact apnigari25@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Security

We use service-provider security controls, encrypted connections, and database access policies to protect information. No online service can guarantee perfect security. Contact us if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

Children

Apni Gari is intended for people aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. Contact us if you believe a child has created an account.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy. We will update the date above and notify users in the app when appropriate.
